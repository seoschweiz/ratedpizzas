Cookie Policy

This policy describes the current use of cookies and similar technologies on RatedPizzas.com.

Current status

RatedPizzas currently does not set its own non-essential cookies and does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or behavioral profiling.

The site is delivered as static pages through GitHub Pages. The hosting and network infrastructure may process technical request information necessary for security, availability and delivery. This is distinct from RatedPizzas placing marketing cookies in your browser.

External links

The site may link to Google Maps, restaurant websites and social or booking services. These are ordinary links rather than embedded services. A third party may use cookies after you choose to visit its website; its own cookie and privacy policies then apply.

Future changes

If optional analytics, advertising or embedded content is added, RatedPizzas will update this policy and implement an appropriate consent choice before non-essential cookies are used where the law requires consent.

Browser controls

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Because RatedPizzas currently has no optional cookie-based functionality, blocking cookies should not materially affect the site.

Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy may be sent to info@diversico.ch. The data controller is Diversico GmbH, Adlikerstrasse 246, 8105 Regensdorf, Switzerland.
